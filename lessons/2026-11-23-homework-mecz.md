## Story

This is the sequel: the match really is on Sunday. Sven checks his phone
first this time. But babcia is coming for lunch and staying into the
evening, and Sven wants to watch quietly. His idea is that Ewa and babcia
read in the kitchen. Babcia wants the match. She knows everything about it
and shouts the whole time, so they cannot hear the TV. Poland score twice,
and babcia sings. Adam asks her to watch next Saturday's match with them.
She can't, because she and Ewa are going to a concert again, but the boys
can come along. From the kitchen Ewa says she has only two tickets.

The -ę/-esz verbs in it: *pije, piją, chce, chcesz, chcę, mogę, możecie,
idziemy*. These are highlighted on the sheet.

**Words to guess** (unglossed): *gol, fantastyczny*. *koncert, mecz, pizza*
were guessed in the lesson. They are now in `vocab.csv` as `queued` and are
not glossed, because he worked them out in the lesson. After this goes out,
add *gol* and *fantastyczny* as `queued` too.

Carded but not yet introduced. **Prioritise these when the story is
approved**: *ciasto, krzyczeć, pomysł*. Storycheck also lists *koncert, mecz,
pizza* as unseen, because it counts cards only. That is expected.

Left out: *często*, which was withdrawn on 2026-09-05 (*Sven często patrzy na
zegar* became *Sven myśli tylko o meczu*). Also left out are *taki, obok, przed,
siebie, zaczynać, tańczyć*, which are not in the deck.

## Questions — what each one needs

- 1, 3, 5, 7: stated in one sentence.
- 2 (*dlaczego nie jest już szczęśliwy*): put together from the next two
  sentences.
- 4 (*o czym myśli Sven*): stated, but in the middle of babcia talking.
- 6 (*dlaczego nie słyszą telewizora*): stated, after the shouting.
- 8 (*dlaczego nie mogą iść na koncert*): inference from Ewa's last line.

It is Sunday and raining, so Marek and Ania are at home. She writes to her
