# Lekcja 2026-10-29 — W pociągu (accusative against instrumental)

Sheet: `out/lessons/2026-10-29-w-pociagu.pdf` (three pages: glosses and
story; the table and ten sentences; eight questions) and
`-opowiadanie.pdf` (story alone).

## Why this

Consolidation, not a new pattern — the teacher's call. The two cases he has
been taught, side by side: *jem rybę* against *chleb z rybą*. Suggested by
the teacher on 2026-10-25, to come once both felt safe alone; the
*Przyjaciel Svena* homework is back (6/8).

An -ę/-isz verb lesson was proposed instead and **parked**: his reading taps
on *śpisz, przychodzisz, jedziesz* point at it, but the teacher wants the
existing patterns solid first. The story still uses -isz/-ę forms
(*mówisz/mówię, słyszysz/słyszę, dzwonisz*) as vocabulary, unexplained.

## Difficult words, rechecked 2026-10-29

By the last six answers per card rather than lifetime misses (the lifetime
list — *chcieć, znaleźć, myć, miesiąc* — is mostly recovered):
*człowiek* and *przyjaciel* production (1/6), *słyszeć* production and *ktoś*
(2/6), then *nagle, zmęczony, późno, tylko, odpowiadać, twarz, łatwy,
spodnie, włączyć*. All in the story except *włączyć*.

## New

*po polsku* and *trochę* — the phrase he will actually use. Nothing else:
his new cards were at 56% that week against 94% for older ones.

## Revised before approval

The first draft had Bartek leave with *Cześć, przyjacielu!*, a *ładna twarz*,
and ended on *mam nowego przyjaciela, i on mówi tylko po polsku* — the
teacher found all three unnatural. Now the man (Marcin, so that *z Marcinem*
is regular — *Bartek* would lose its e) says Sven looks tired and gives him
chocolate, and it ends *Z kim? — Nie wiem. Ale ma dobrą czekoladę.*

## Exercise

Sixteen sentences (ten at first; the teacher asked for more), noun in
brackets, eight accusative and eight instrumental; seven ona, four on, five
ono. Opens with the teacher's pair, *ryba* twice, so the contrast is the
first thing he sees. Three answers do not change (*mleko*, *okno*,
*śniadanie*) — the job is choosing, so not every item can change.

Answers: rybę, rybą, brata, krzesłem, książkę, rowerem, mleko, siostrą,
chlebem, okno, kobietę, śniadanie, ławką, domem, drzewem, wodę.

## What to watch

**Feminine adjectives end in -ą in the accusative too**: *zmęczoną twarz,
dobrą czekoladę* in the story look like instrumental endings and are not.
Not in the table (nouns only); worth a sentence if he asks.
