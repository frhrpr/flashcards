# Lekcja — być / mieć + pytania

**2026-08-09** · conversational, no writing, ~35 min ·
sheet: `Downloads/flashcards/lessons/2026-08-09-byc-miec-pytania.html`

Grammar lesson rather than vocabulary. Prompted by the progress data: `być`
(11 of 18 missed) and `mieć` (6 of 14) are two of his four worst cards, and
both sit at the ease floor.

**Why the cards cannot fix this.** The deck teaches `być` as a headword, but he
will never say `być` — he will say *jestem*, *jest*, *są*. The card is drilling
a label disconnected from every form he actually meets. Connecting the two is
oral work.

## Plan

1. **Forms in the mouth** (5 min) — pronoun → verb, no explanation, then reversed,
   then interleaved. Three persons only: ja / ty / on / ona.
2. **Czy questions, both directions** (7 min) — he answers, then *he asks*. He has
   never practised forming a question; everything so far has had him answering.
3. **The negative** (5 min) — `nie mam` + genitive. Use food words, since animate
   masculine hides the case (*psa* looks the same either way).
4. **Open questions with objects** (10 min) — use the room, pick things up.
5. **Information gap** (8 min) — "find out three things about me, only questions
   with *jest* or *masz*". Then stop helping.
6. **Round-up** — he describes the room unprompted.
