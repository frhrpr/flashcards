# Lekcja 2026-10-25 — Kawa z mlekiem (z + instrumental, at the restaurant)

Sheet: `out/lessons/2026-10-25-kawa-z-mlekiem.pdf` (three pages: glosses and
story; the two tables and nine sentences; eight questions) and
`-opowiadanie.pdf` (story alone). On the Reading page in the app.

## Why this

His vocabulary was in good shape on 2026-10-25: only five words under 50% on
their last eight answers (*twarz, człowiek, włączyć, słyszeć, spodnie*), all
but one brand new and covered by the *Ważny dzień* homework. So the lesson
moves grammar on instead of repairing words.

*z* + instrumental is the next step in *Hurra!!! Po polsku 1* (after -m/-sz
verbs and the accusative, both done), and the most regular case ending in
Polish: **-em** for on and ono, **-ą** for ona. He already uses it in fixed
phrases (*nad rzeką, pod drzewem, płaci kartą, chleb z serem*); this names it.

Topic: the restaurant, because the phrases are the grammar (*kawa z mlekiem,
chleb z masłem, herbata z cytryną*).

## Kept out on purpose — irregular, for a later lesson

- the vanishing e: *pies → psem, cukier → cukrem, dziadek → dziadkiem,
  ojciec → ojcem, mąż → mężem* (so: lemon in the tea, not sugar)
- *ze* before clusters: *ze Svenem, ze mną* (so Sven is never "with")
- pronouns *ze mną, z tobą, z nim, z nią*; the plural *-ami*
- **the other z**: *człowiek z parku* in his last homework is "from" and takes
  a different case. Worth one sentence if he asks.

In: the k/g rule (*mleko → z mlekiem*), as a note in the table, because
*kawa z mlekiem* is too useful to avoid. Not in the exercise.

## New words

Menu: *pierogi, ryż, czekolada, cytryna* (not in the deck; card after the
lesson if they stick), *mięso* (carded, unseen). The restaurant itself:
*restauracja, ile, kosztować*. Arriving on his cards this week anyway:
*babcia, dlaczego, ktoś, nagle, nic, przychodzić, razem, szczęśliwy, zmęczony*.

Vocabulary he is shaky on, worked in where it fits: *człowiek, otwierać,
blisko, można, głodny*.

## Exercise

Nine sentences, the noun in brackets, three per gender, no two of one gender
in a row, every noun one he has met, no answer appearing in the story. Varied
on purpose (asked 2026-10-25): the gap at the start, middle or end; after
*z* (5), *pod* (2), *nad* (1), and once with nothing before it — *jedzie do
pracy rowerem*, the "how" use he already knows from *płaci kartą*.

Answers: bratem, miastem, ławką, rowerem, krzesłem, żoną, przyjacielem,
pytaniem, siostrą.

## Revised 2026-10-25, before the lesson

First draft had him ordering bread and butter and soup with bread, rain
nobody could be sure of (*chyba pada deszcz*), soup "still hot" for no
reason, and *kosztuje dużo, ale babcia płaci* with no contrast to justify
*ale*. Now: fish with rice, pierogi with meat, ice cream with chocolate, and
*— To nic! — mówi babcia i płaci kartą.* The ending is unchanged.
