## Why this

Homework after *Kawa z mlekiem*: the same grammar, *z* + instrumental with
regular endings, and the restaurant words from the lesson, which are all new
to him and so all count as difficult. *przyjaciel* four times in three forms,
at the teacher's request — it gave him trouble in the lesson. Also from his
trouble list: *wysoki, twarz, słyszeć, otwierać, wiedzieć, daleko, jeszcze*.

New: *miło mi*, *u nas*, *naleśniki* (glossed). *dzwonić* is carded but was
unseen; prioritised.

## Revised before approval

The first draft repeated the lesson (fish with rice, ice cream with
chocolate, *To nic!* at the bill) and had Adam panic about his bike in the
rain. Now Sven has pancakes, Ewa just soup, Ewa offers *Dzisiaj śpisz u nas*,
and Sven says *Dzisiaj ja płacę!*. The teacher also noted that *to nic* at
the bill is not common in real life — don't reach for it again.
