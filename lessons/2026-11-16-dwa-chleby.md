# Lekcja 2026-11-16 — Dwa chleby (mixed revision: both cases, both verb groups)

Sheet: `out/lessons/2026-11-16-dwa-chleby.pdf` (three pages: glosses and
story; eight questions; the two tables and twelve sentences to build) and
`-opowiadanie.pdf` (story alone).

## Why this

A refresher rather than a new topic, after two lessons on verbs. He never did
the *Gość Marcina* homework, so this is the first time since 29 October he has
had to choose between the accusative and the instrumental. His case grammar
cards were clean on 2026-11-15; the point is to keep them so, not to repair
them. The locative was offered and deliberately not chosen.

First drafted as accusative against instrumental only. The teacher then asked
for everything taught so far, mixed together: the two cases and the two
present-tense groups (*-am* from *Szukam psa!*, *-ę/-isz* from *Urodziny
Ewy*). Interleaving is the right shape for a revision: in speech he has to
decide which rule applies, and an exercise on one rule makes that decision
for him. The cue tells him whether a gap is a noun or a verb, so the real
choices are the case, and the verb group plus the person.

The *-ę/-esz* verbs (*chcę, mogę, piję, idę, jadę*) remain the next new topic.

## Story

Piotr, Ewa's brother, comes to dinner with his wife Ola. Sven finds there is no
bread, runs to the shop, comes back with butter on his shirt, finds his other
shirt under the bed — and Piotr arrives carrying bread. They end up with two
loaves.

The dialogue was extended for the verbs. Three colours on the sheet: amber for
the accusative (*zupę, rybę, koszulę, Ewę, Olę, na Piotra, na Svena*), blue
for the instrumental (*z żoną Olą, z chlebem i z masłem, pod łóżkiem, z
masłem*) and purple for the verb endings:

- *-am*: *mamy, czekam, masz, mam, pamiętasz, mają*;
- *-ę/-isz*: *słyszę, robisz, dzwonimy, słyszycie, lubimy, patrzą*, and *mówi,
  patrzy, dzwoni* in narration.

*Słyszę! Nie mów tak głośno!* carries on the running joke from *Urodziny Ewy*
and *Babcia Ewy*. *Ola i ja dzwonimy* is the *X i ja = my* trap. Piotr rings
the bell and then phones (*dzwonić* in both senses).

Ewa's line about the shirt went through three versions. *Teraz Sven ma brudną
koszulę* was cut because accusative *-ą* on an adjective looks exactly like
the instrumental. *Koszula jest brudna!* was then too stiff for the teacher:
nobody says it. It is now *Sven, co ty robisz? Masz masło na koszuli!*, which
is what a Pole would say. *na koszuli* is a locative, not yet taught, so it is
glossed as a phrase on the sheet. It is one form in the reading text and
appears in neither the table nor the exercise.

Problem words: *obiad, koszula, biec, nieść, inny, głośno, dzwonić,
przychodzić, znaleźć, myć, późno, pamiętać, czysty*. Nothing new.

## Exercise — build a sentence

The teacher's idea, 2026-11-16: instead of gaps, he gets the dictionary forms
(*ja / czytać / książka*) and writes the sentence. It is a step up, from
choosing one ending to producing a whole sentence, and it fits a mixed revision
because almost every item needs a verb ending and a case at once. The gapped
version (sixteen items) is kept in the scratchpad if it is wanted back.

Rules for the prompts:

- Only verbs from the two groups taught: *pić, jeść, iść* would test stems
  he has not been given.
- Prepositions are given (*z, na, pod*): the point is the ending after them,
  not choosing them.
- One worked example, taken from the story.
- Twelve items rather than sixteen, because building is slower than filling a
  gap.

| | prompt | answer | verb | case |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ex. | Piotr i Ola / patrzeć / na / Sven | Piotr i Ola patrzą na Svena. | | |
| 1 | ja / czytać / książka | Czytam książkę. | -am, ja | acc |
| 2 | Sven / lubić / ryż / z / mięso | Sven lubi ryż z mięsem. | -isz, on | acc (no change), ins |
| 3 | babcia / mieszkać / z / kot | Babcia mieszka z kotem. | -am, ona | ins |
| 4 | ty / patrzeć / na / ptak | Patrzysz na ptaka. | -isz, ty (-y) | acc, living |
| 5 | my / czekać / na / autobus | Czekamy na autobus. | -am, my | acc (no change) |
| 6 | wy / robić / zupa / z / ryba | Robicie zupę z rybą. | -isz, wy | acc, ins |
| 7 | Ola / mieć / brat / i / siostra | Ola ma brata i siostrę. | -am, ona | acc ×2, living |
| 8 | oni / słyszeć / samochód / pod / okno | Słyszą samochód pod oknem. | -isz, oni (-y) | acc (no change), ins |
| 9 | wy / pytać / kelner | Pytacie kelnera. | -am, wy | acc, living |
| 10 | my / mówić / po polsku / z / kelner | Mówimy po polsku z kelnerem. | -isz, my | ins |
| 11 | oni / pamiętać / Ola | Pamiętają Olę. | -am, oni | acc |
| 12 | ja / robić / herbata / z / cytryna | Robię herbatę z cytryną. | -isz, ja | acc, ins |

Each group gets six items. *-ę/-isz* has all six persons, *-am* every person
but *ty* (the story gives *masz, pamiętasz*). Nine accusatives (two
unchanged, four living) and six instrumentals across all three genders.
Accept the pronoun kept or dropped, and any sensible word order.

None of the twelve verb forms is in the story. The example is copied from it
on purpose: the first item is the model, not a test.

## What to watch

- **9 and 10** are *kelner* twice, *pytacie kelnera* against *z kelnerem*.
- **5 and 8** are the no-change accusatives, a thing after *na* and a thing
  as object; he may want to change them.
- **4 and 8**: *patrzysz, słyszą* take the *-y* spelling.
- **7**: both nouns change, *brata i siostrę*.
- **11 against 3**: *pamiętają* and *mieszka*; oni *-ją* in the *-am* group,
  where the *-ę/-isz* group has *-ą* (8).
