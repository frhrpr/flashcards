# Homework 2026-10-29 — Gość Marcina (reading, accusative against instrumental)

Sheet: `out/lessons/2026-10-29-homework-gosc-marcina.pdf` (three pages:
glosses and story; table and sixteen sentences; eight questions) and
`-opowiadanie.pdf` (story alone, for email). For the Reading page once
approved.

## Why this

Homework after *W pociągu*: the same contrast, *kupuje rybę* against *lody z
czekoladą*, and the same difficult words (*człowiek, przyjaciel, słyszeć,
ktoś, nagle, zmęczony, późno, tylko, odpowiadać, twarz, łatwy*),
plus *włączyć* as an imperative, *włącz lampę!*, which the lesson story had
no room for. Marcin from the lesson comes to dinner and brings Robert, who
turns out to be the waiter from *Przyjaciel Svena* — Ewa remembers that he
saw her eat only soup.

Nothing new. *trochę* from the lesson is reused.

Revised before approval: the friend first arrived as *wysoki człowiek w
czarnych spodniach*, an unintended echo of the man in red trousers the day
before; Marcin's chocolate went straight into *Lody z czekoladą!*, which read
oddly; and it ended *Dzisiaj pan nie pracuje!*, which confused the teacher.
Now Marcin comes *z przyjacielem*, they eat ice cream and Ewa has hers with
his chocolate, and the ending says outright that Robert works in a restaurant
and so always says *Smacznego!*. *spodnie* is no longer in it.

The friend was first called Jan; storycheck refused it, because *jan* is
spelled like a word in the lists. *wszyscy* was cut because the reading page
would map it to the *wszystko* card and gloss it "everything".

## Exercise

Sixteen sentences, all new nouns against the lesson's, eight accusative and
eight instrumental. *Ewa mówi z kelnerem* and *Ewa ma nowego przyjaciela*
echo the story without giving any answer away.

Answers: kurtkę, kelnerem, ptaka, drzewem, kartę, cytryną, przyjaciela,
czekoladą, krzesło, miastem, list, kobietą, lodówkę, sklepem, okno, rzeką.
