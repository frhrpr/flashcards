# Homework 2026-11-10 — Babcia Ewy (reading, the -ę / -isz verbs)

Sheet: `out/lessons/2026-11-10-homework-babcia-ewy.pdf` (three pages:
glosses and story; eight questions; table and sixteen sentences) and
`-opowiadanie.pdf` (story alone, for email). For the Reading page once
approved. Listening episode 4, *Deszcz*, goes with it.

## Why this

Homework after *Urodziny Ewy*, today's verbs only — the teacher's call. He
did not do *Gość Marcina*; the cases come back as refreshers in later lessons
rather than piled onto this one.

The lesson's model verbs were *mówić* and *słyszeć*, so here the story carries
*robić* and *lubić* in all six persons and the exercise gaps *mówić, słyszeć,
dzwonić, myśleć, patrzeć, uczyć się* — the verbs he now has a table for.

Ewa warns Sven that her grandmother does not hear well, so he shouts; the
grandmother concludes that Sven is the one who cannot hear, and from then on
both shout at each other while Ewa laughs in the kitchen. Same family of joke
as Adam in the lesson, on purpose: *głośno* and *słyszeć* again.

Problem words: *człowiek, słyszeć, dzwonić, przychodzić, głośno, nieść,
wtedy, łatwy*. Nothing new. *niedziela* was drafted and swapped for
*poniedziałek*: carded but unseen.

## Exercise — answers

słyszę, dzwonisz, mówimy, uczycie się, mówią, myślę, patrzy, słyszycie,
słyszymy, patrzysz, uczą się, mówicie, mówię, dzwonimy, myślicie, słyszą.

Three each for *ja, my, oni*, four for *wy* (the ending he will hesitate on
most), two for *ty*, one for *on* (the forms the story already uses most). Half
are *-y* verbs. Checked mechanically: all distinct, none in the story, none
printed in another item. **Item 3, *Ewa i ja*, is *my* again.**
